About me

I'm a DevOps & AI/ML Engineer who likes systems that don't wake anyone up at 3 a.m. My work centers on cloud infrastructure automation on Azure and AWS — provisioning with Terraform, containerizing with Docker, orchestrating with Kubernetes, and wiring it all together with Jenkins and GitHub Actions pipelines that build, test, and ship on their own.

Alongside infrastructure, I'm building depth in Machine Learning and Generative AI — from supervised/unsupervised learning fundamentals to LLMs, RAG, embeddings, and semantic search — with an eye toward MLOps: getting models deployed, monitored, and maintained with the same rigor as any other production service.
